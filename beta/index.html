Beta Program

From side project to Play Store. Help test early builds.

04.1 / join

How to join

Three steps, about two minutes. You get new builds before everyone else, straight from Google Play.

~/beta/README.mdmd

  1. Join the Google Group

    Use the same Google account you use on the Play Store. Membership is what unlocks the beta builds.

    Join Google Group ↗

  2. Pick your apps

    Choose apps below. Tap Web opt-in on a laptop, or Play Store beta on your phone, then install or update the app.

  3. Leave anytime

    Open the app in the Play Store, scroll to Beta program and tap Leave. You go back to the public version.

// What you sign up for.
const beta = BetaProgram(
  group: 'aymorato-apps-tester',
  builds: Track.early,     // before public release
  feedback: Channel.email, // aymorato.apps@gmail.com
  cost: Price.free,
  leave: Anytime(),
);
void main() => beta.join();
