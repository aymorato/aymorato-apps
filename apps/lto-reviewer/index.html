1 About

Prepare for the Philippine driver's license written exam with LTO Reviewer. Practice in English or Filipino, learn from clear explanations, and study offline wherever you are.

What's inside

  • 1,000 practice questions covering regulatory, warning and informative signs, rules of the road, and safe driving
  • Non-Professional and Professional tracks, with 150 additional questions for Professional
  • Mock exam with 60 randomized items, a 60-minute timer, and a 48/60 passing score
  • Custom exams with your choice of categories, 10 to 60 items, and a timer
  • Practice mode with instant explanations, plus a review of your mistakes
  • Bilingual study with questions and explanations in English and Filipino
  • Sign Library with more than 190 road signs and their meanings
  • Progress tracking by category, weakest topics, and exam history
  • Works offline with questions, signs, and progress stored on your phone

Disclaimer: LTO Reviewer is an independent educational reviewer and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing the Land Transportation Office (LTO) or any government agency. It does not provide government services, book exams, or issue or renew licenses or permits. Practice questions are for study purposes and are not actual LTO exam questions. Rules and requirements can change; confirm current information with official sources.
