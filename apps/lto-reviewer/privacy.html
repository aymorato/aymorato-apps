Privacy Policy for LTO Reviewer

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The App has no sign-up, login, or account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or any other personal data. Your answers, scores, and progress are stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, measure, and prevent fraud on ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as ads viewed and tapped, and diagnostic data about ad performance

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies; we do not receive or store it. Learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your Ad Choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. On any device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings under Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads). Buying "Remove Ads" stops all ads in the App.

In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play under Google's policies. We never see your payment details. The App receives only a purchase confirmation from Google Play, which is stored on your device to unlock the purchase.

Permissions Used

  • Internet: to load ads and to connect to Google Play for purchases. Questions, exams, and progress work fully offline.
  • Network state: to detect when you are back online so ads can load.
  • Billing: to make the in-app purchase through Google Play.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

Data Storage, Security, and Deletion

Your study data never leaves your device and is not stored on any server of ours. Data sent to Google by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play is sent over encrypted connections. To delete your study data, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.

Children

The App is intended for people preparing for a driver's license exam and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always available in the App under Settings > Privacy Policy, with the date of the last update at the top.

Disclaimer

This app is provided "as is" without any warranties of any kind. The developer is not responsible for any damages, losses, or issues arising from the use of this application. Users assume full responsibility for how they use the app.

Affiliation

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Land Transportation Office (LTO) of the Philippines or any government agency. "LTO" refers to the Land Transportation Office and is used only to describe the driver's license exam this app helps you prepare for. All exam information is sourced from public domains: lto.gov.ph and officialgazette.gov.ph.

Source of Content

Review materials are original summaries inspired by public LTO driver's license exam coverage. This is an unofficial reviewer tool.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: aymorato.apps@gmail.com
