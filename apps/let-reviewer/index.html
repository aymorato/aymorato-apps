1 About

Mabuhay, future LPT!

LET Reviewer is your study partner for the Licensure Examination for Teachers (LET) in the Philippines. Pangarap mong maging Licensed Professional Teacher? Simulan na natin. Review anytime, anywhere - sa jeep, sa break, o bago matulog.

What's inside

  • 1,100 LET-style questions, each with a clear rationale so you learn the "why", not just the answer
  • General Education (500 items): English, Mathematics, Science, Filipino, Social Science
  • Professional Education (600 items): Child & Adolescent Development, Facilitating Learning, Assessment of Learning, Curriculum Development, Guidance & Counseling, and The Teaching Profession & Education Laws

Features

  • Create Your Exam - pick subjects, number of items and a time limit; every attempt draws a fresh set
  • Mock LET Board Exam - 100 items (40 Gen Ed + 60 Prof Ed) with a 2-hour timer
  • Flag & Zoom - flag tricky items to review before submitting, and enlarge the text
  • Improve Where You Struggle - every answer is tracked by topic, so you see your weakest areas and drill them
  • Daily 10, bookmarks, exam history, score trend, study streak and XP levels

Who is it for

  • Education graduates (BEEd, BSEd) taking the LET for the first time
  • Repeaters who want to focus on their weak topics
  • Career shifters and teachers taking professional education units

"Teaching is the greatest act of optimism." Every item you answer brings you closer to the three letters after your name: LPT. Kaya mo 'yan!

Questions are for review purposes only and are not actual board exam items.
