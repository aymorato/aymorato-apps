Privacy Policy for LET Reviewer 2027: Exam Reviewer

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The App has no sign-up, login, or account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

Permissions Used

Internet and network state permissions are used only for loading ads and checking if you are online. All questions work offline. The advertising ID is used only by Google AdMob, and Google Play Billing is used only for the optional in-app purchase.

Data Storage/Security

All data is stored locally on your device, including your exam history, scores, bookmarks, and settings. The App does not send this data to us or to anyone else. You can delete it anytime by clearing data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

Advertising

This app shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as your advertising ID and IP address. This process is handled entirely by Google under its own policies (https://policies.google.com/privacy), and we do not receive or store this data.

In-App Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are available to remove ads and unlock premium features. These purchases are processed securely by Google Play and are not handled by us directly.

Disclaimer

This app is provided "as is" without any warranties of any kind. The developer is not responsible for any damages, losses, or issues arising from the use of this application. Users assume full responsibility for how they use the app.

Affiliation

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), the Board for Professional Teachers, the Department of Education (DepEd) of the Philippines, or any government agency. "LET" refers to the Licensure Examination for Teachers and is used only to describe the exam this app helps you prepare for. For exam schedules, applications, and results, please refer to the PRC at prc.gov.ph.

Source of Content

All practice questions and rationales are original items written for this app, based on the publicly known coverage of the LET (General Education and Professional Education) and on public laws such as RA 7836 and RA 10533 (K to 12), available at prc.gov.ph, officialgazette.gov.ph, and deped.gov.ph. They are not actual LET board exam questions. This is an unofficial reviewer tool.
