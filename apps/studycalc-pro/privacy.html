Privacy Policy for StudyCalc Pro

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how StudyCalc Pro (package name: com.aymorato.studycalcpro) handles information when you use the app. By using StudyCalc Pro, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

StudyCalc Pro does not collect personal data. There is no sign-up, no account, and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, school, or any other personal information.

Everything you create in the app (calculations, history, folders, graphs, bookmarked formulas, study notes, settings, and progress) stays on your device. We do not receive, see, or store it on any server.

Permissions Used

  • Internet: used only to load ads in the free version and to process purchases through Google Play Billing. All calculator, graphing, converter, formula, and study features work fully offline.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used to check whether you are online, so ads load only when a connection is available.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads in the free version.
  • Google Play Billing (BILLING): used to process the optional one-time Pro purchase.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos, or files.

Data Storage and Security

All app data is stored locally on your device. We operate no servers and maintain no database of user information.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, your app data may be included in your own Google account backup, which Google manages under its own terms. Uninstalling the app deletes its data from your device.

When you choose to export your history, notes, graphs, or PDFs, the file is created on your device and shared only to the destination you select.

Advertising

The free version of StudyCalc Pro shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's Advertising ID, other device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, and app interaction data (such as ad views and clicks), in accordance with Google's policies.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your Privacy Choices (GDPR / US State Privacy Laws)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy laws (such as California), the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent or show your privacy choices before any ads are requested. Where no consent has been given, no ads are loaded. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy options.

Ads are limited to content rated for teens and are never shown during Exam Mode. Buying Pro removes all ads, and the ad SDK is not started for Pro users.

In-App Purchases

StudyCalc Pro offers an optional one-time purchase (Pro) that removes ads and unlocks extra features. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details (such as card, GCash, or other payment information). Google Play only tells the app whether Pro has been purchased. Google Play's privacy practices are described at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Disclaimer

StudyCalc Pro is provided "as is" and "as available", without warranties of any kind, express or implied, including accuracy, fitness for a particular purpose, or non-infringement. The app is a study aid; always double-check important results. We are not liable for any loss or damage arising from the use of the app.

Affiliation

StudyCalc Pro is not affiliated with the Department of Education (DepEd), the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), or any school, education ministry or exam board. It is an independent utility tool for students to practice and check their work. For official grades and requirements, refer to your school.

Source of Content

Calculations are performed by the app's own original calculation engine. Formulas in the formula library are standard formulas from public educational standards in mathematics, science, and engineering, written and explained in our own words.

Children's Privacy

StudyCalc Pro is intended for students aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact Us
