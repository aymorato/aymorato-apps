StudyCalc Pro

Offline scientific calculator with graphs, formulas and step-by-step explanations. No login needed.

▶ Google Play — Coming Soon Privacy Policy

1 About

A fast, beautiful study calculator that works 100% offline. No account and no login: just open it and start solving. StudyCalc Pro brings a scientific calculator, a grapher, a unit converter and a formula library together in one app for students.

What's inside

  • Smart calculator with a multi-line worksheet, live preview, memory keys (MC, MR, M+, M−) and Ans
  • Scientific functions: sin, cos, tan and their inverses, hyperbolic functions, log, ln, eˣ, 10ˣ, √, ∛, powers, factorial, π, e, |x|, mod and percent, in degree or radian mode
  • Step-by-step explanations that show how the answer was worked out and recognize topics like the Pythagorean identity, all offline with no AI service
  • Graphing of functions like y = x² + 2x + 1, with pinch-zoom, pan, tap-to-trace, roots, y-intercept and the derivative
  • Formula library of 70 formulas for algebra, geometry, trigonometry, statistics, physics, chemistry and engineering, each with a worked example
  • Unit converter for length, weight, volume, temperature, area, speed, energy, pressure and time
  • Student tools: formula of the day, quick-review flashcards and study notes saved on your device
  • Exam Mode, a plain calculator with history and hints locked
  • Progress with weekly activity charts, daily streaks, levels from Student to Master, and badges
  • History with search, favorites and folders

Free or Pro

The free version includes every calculator function, unlimited history, 3 graphs and 10 formula bookmarks, with light ads. StudyCalc Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase with no ads, unlimited graphs and bookmarks, advanced themes, PDF export and priority support.

Disclaimer: StudyCalc Pro is not affiliated with DepEd, CHED or any school, education ministry or exam board. It is an independent utility for students to practice and check their work. For official grades and requirements, refer to your school.
