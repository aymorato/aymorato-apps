1 About

Preparing for the Philippine Civil Service Examination? CSC Mastery helps you review smarter with 1,100 exam-style practice questions, full timed mock exams, and a clear explanation for every answer.

Whether you are taking the Professional or Sub-Professional level, CSC Mastery lets you practice anytime, track your progress, and focus on the topics you need most.

What's inside

  • 1,100 practice questions in 6 categories – Verbal, Numerical, Analytical, Clerical, General Information and Abstract Reasoning
  • Learn from every answer – a detailed explanation for every question
  • Full timed mock exams – up to 170 items with a countdown timer, flagging and item navigation
  • Create your own exams – pick subjects, number of items, time limit and difficulty
  • Instant results – score, 80% passing mark check and accuracy per subject
  • Track your progress – exam history, score trend and your weakest topics
  • No account needed – your scores and history stay on your phone

Disclaimer: CSC Mastery is an independent review app. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Civil Service Commission (CSC) of the Philippines or any government agency. All questions are original practice items written for review purposes. For official information, visit csc.gov.ph.
