BuilderPro: Estimates & Costs

Construction estimator for materials, costs, projects and PDF reports. Works fully offline.

▶ Google Play — Coming Soon Privacy Policy

1 About

BuilderPro is an offline construction estimation app for contractors, engineers, foremen, quantity surveyors, architects and homeowners. Compute the materials a job needs, see what they cost using your own prices, save everything to a project, and hand your client a clean PDF report, all on your phone and even without signal on site.

Every estimate follows one simple workflow: calculate, estimate cost, save to a project, then generate a report. Results show the actual items you buy (cement bags, sand, gravel, 6 m deformed bars, tie wire, hollow blocks, tiles, paint, roofing sheets and screws), each priced from your active price template.

What's inside

  • Material cost estimator with quantity × unit price, waste allowance, labor % and markup % in one grand total
  • 8 estimators: Concrete, Slab, Rebar (slab, beam, column, footing), Column, CHB Wall, Paint, Tile and Roofing
  • 9 price templates including NCR Average, Provincial Average and Budget Build; edit any price or create your own supplier list
  • Projects with total cost, cost by estimate type and a complete bill of materials
  • PDF reports with project info, materials list, cost breakdown and grand total
  • Unit converter for length, area, volume, weight, pressure and force (metric ↔ imperial, board feet)
  • Engineering formula library for civil, structural, electrical and mechanical work, with search and bookmarks
  • Made for Filipino builders: peso prices, CHB 4" and 6", long span and corrugated roofing, 40 kg cement bags, and English or Tagalog menus
  • 9 currencies: PHP, USD, EUR, GBP, INR, AED, SGD, CAD and AUD, with offline exchange rates
  • Works offline with unlimited history, backup and restore to a file you keep, and dark mode

Free and Premium

BuilderPro is free with ads and includes up to 5 projects, 3 PDF reports per month and 2 custom price templates. BuilderPro Premium is a one-time purchase through Google Play, not a subscription. It removes all ads and unlocks unlimited projects, PDF reports and price templates, advanced reports (company header, labor, contingency and signature lines) and premium themes.

Disclaimer: BuilderPro is a productivity and educational tool provided as-is. Estimates are planning guides based on the dimensions, standard estimating factors and prices you enter; always verify quantities against approved plans, specifications and local building codes before purchasing or building. Default prices are typical 2026 market estimates and can be edited. BuilderPro is an independent app and is not affiliated with any government agency.
