Privacy Policy for GWA Calculator PH

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The App has no sign-up, login, or account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

Permissions Used

Internet permission is used only for loading ads and for processing the optional in-app purchase through Google Play. All GWA calculations work offline.

Data Storage/Security

All data (subjects, units, grades, saved semesters, and settings) is stored locally on your device. No personal data is transmitted externally. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. When you tap "Share", the App creates an image of your GWA card on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to share it with.

Advertising

This app shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as your Advertising ID. This process is handled entirely by Google under its own policies (https://policies.google.com/privacy), and we do not receive or store this data. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads.

In-App Purchases

An optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is available to turn off all ads. This purchase is processed securely by Google Play and is not handled by us directly. We never receive your payment details.

Disclaimer

This app is provided "as is" without any warranties of any kind. The developer is not responsible for any damages, losses, or issues arising from the use of this application. Users assume full responsibility for how they use the app.

Affiliation

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), the Department of Education (DepEd), any government agency, or any school or university in the Philippines. It is an independent app only and does not provide government services or issue academic records. For official education information, visit ched.gov.ph and deped.gov.ph.

Source of Content

The GWA is computed with the standard weighted average formula: the sum of (grade × units) divided by the total units. The 1.0–5.0 grading scale, percent equivalents, and Latin honor ranges shown in the App are general guides based on scales commonly used by Philippine colleges and universities. They are estimates only, as each school sets its own rules. Always confirm your final GWA and honor eligibility with your school's Registrar.
