Offline GWA calculator for PH students with Latin honors and what-if grades.

1 About

GWA Calculator PH is the fastest, cleanest way for Filipino college students to compute their General Weighted Average (GWA). Built for the Philippine 1.0 – 5.0 grading system used by most CHED-recognized colleges and universities, it works 100% offline and keeps everything on your phone.

Type your subjects, set the units, pick your grades, and your GWA updates instantly. No sign-up, no internet needed for calculations.

Why students love it

  • Instant GWA – live, accurate weighted average with 2 decimals
  • Latin Honor checker – see if you're on track for Summa, Magna or Cum Laude
  • Equivalent percentage – estimated percent for your GWA
  • INC support – incomplete grades are excluded from your GWA but still counted in total units
  • What-If planner – "What grade do I need to get a 1.50 GWA?" answered in one tap
  • Semester history – save every semester and see your overall (cumulative) GWA
  • Share your GWA card – generate a beautiful image for your group chat or socials
  • Works offline – all calculations run on your device
  • Light & dark mode – easy on the eyes during late-night review sessions

Latin honor guide used by the app

  • Summa Cum Laude: 1.00 – 1.20
  • Magna Cum Laude: 1.21 – 1.45
  • Cum Laude: 1.46 – 1.75

Disclaimer: GWA Calculator PH is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to CHED or any school or university. Grading systems, percentage equivalents and Latin honor requirements differ per school. Always confirm your official GWA and honor eligibility with your Registrar.
