Lista Mo - Utang Tracker

Offline utang ledger and sari-sari POS. No login, and your data stays on your phone.

▶ Google Play — Coming Soon Privacy Policy

1 About

Ilipat na ang kuwaderno ng utang sa phone mo. Lista Mo is a simple, offline ledger for sari-sari stores and small sellers: record utang, track bayad, see who is overdue, and sell faster with a built-in POS. There is no account, no sign-up and no password, and your records are saved only on your phone.

What's inside

  • Customers with name, phone, address and an optional 1x1 photo, plus search and filters for balance and overdue
  • Utang with items (qty × price auto-totals), due date shortcuts (+7 / +15 / +30 days) and an optional receipt photo
  • Bayad for partial or full payments by Cash, GCash or Other; payments apply to the oldest utang first
  • Overdue badges in red, and orange DUE TODAY badges, so you know who to collect from
  • Dashboard with total receivable, to collect today, overdue, collected this week and sales today
  • Sari-sari POS with a product grid, quick one-off items, a cart, automatic sukli, or charge the cart to a customer's utang
  • Paluwagan tracker with groups, payout order, per-round hulog status and SMS reminders
  • Reminders in one tap with balance, due date and your GCash number, via SMS or share
  • Export your list of balances as PDF or CSV
  • Tagalog or English, with light and dark mode

Free and Pro

The free version supports up to 20 customers and shows ads. Lista Mo Pro is a one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks unlimited customers and PDF/CSV exports.
