Privacy Policy for Lista Mo - Utang Tracker

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Lista Mo - Utang Tracker (package name: com.aymorato.listamo; "the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is an offline record-keeping tool for sari-sari stores and small sellers. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

  • We do not collect personal data. There is no sign-up, no account and no login.
  • Information you enter in the App (customer names, phone numbers, addresses, GCash numbers, photos, utang, payments, sales, products and paluwagan records) stays on your device. We never receive, upload or see it.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect certain device information on its own, as described under "Advertising" below.

Permissions Used

  • Internet / Network state: only to load ads (free version) and to connect to Google Play Billing for the optional Pro purchase. The App's features work without internet.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): used by Google AdMob to show ads in the free version.
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): used only for the optional Pro purchase.
  • Camera / Photos: the App does not request a camera or storage permission. If you choose to add a customer photo or receipt photo, the App opens your phone's camera or photo picker, and the selected photo is saved only inside the App on your device.
  • SMS, phone calls and sharing: the App does not request SMS or phone permissions. Reminders and calls open your phone's own SMS, dialer or share apps, and nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

Data Storage and Security

  • All App data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate any server that stores your records.
  • Your records may be included in your phone's own Android backup (Google account backup) if you have backup turned on, so they can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google and your device settings, not by us.
  • PDF/CSV exports are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them.
  • You can delete your data at any time by deleting entries in the App, clearing the App's data, or uninstalling the App.
  • Keep your phone secured with a screen lock, since anyone with access to your unlocked phone can open the App.

Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and use the Advertising ID, other device identifiers, IP address (approximate location), app interactions and diagnostic data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, in accordance with Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Users who buy Pro do not see ads, and the ad SDK is not started for them.

In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time purchase (Lista Mo Pro) that removes ads and unlocks unlimited customers and exports. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card, GCash or other payment details. Google Play's terms and privacy policy apply to purchases.

Source of Content

All records in the App are user-generated: they are entered by you and stored locally on your device. We do not provide, review or verify this content, and you are responsible for the accuracy of the records you keep.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users 18 years old and above and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Disclaimer

The App is provided "as is" and "as available", without warranties of any kind, express or implied. We are not responsible for lost, incorrect or incomplete records, including data lost when the App is uninstalled, the device is reset or lost, or backup is turned off. The App is a record-keeping tool only. It does not lend money, collect debts, or process or send payments.

Affiliation

Lista Mo is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to GCash, Mynt, any bank, lending company or government agency. GCash is a trademark of its respective owner and is mentioned only to describe the payment method you record.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date will be updated.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: aymorato.apps@gmail.com
