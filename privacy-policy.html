Privacy Policy – CSC Mastery

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the CSC Mastery mobile application ("the App," "we," "us") handles information. CSC Mastery is a review app for the Philippine Civil Service Examination. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Civil Service Commission or any government agency.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The App has no sign-up, login, or account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves the following only on your own device:

  • your exam history and scores,
  • your answers in past exams, used for the Analytics page,
  • custom exams you create,
  • your app settings, and a record of your CSC Mastery Pro purchase (so ads stay removed).

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete your exam history at any time from the History page. Uninstalling the App deletes all of this data.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party service by Google LLC. To show and measure ads, Google may automatically collect certain information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system and app version),
  • ad interactions (such as ads viewed or tapped).

This collection is done by Google under its own policies, not by us. We do not receive or store this information. To learn how Google uses this information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time purchase, "CSC Mastery Pro," which removes ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. We never see or store your payment details or Google account details. Google Play gives the App a purchase confirmation, which is saved only on your device so your purchase is remembered and can be restored.

5. Internet Access

The App's questions and features work without an internet connection. The App uses the internet only to load ads and to make or restore purchases through Google Play.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adults and students preparing for the Civil Service Examination and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Data Security

Because we do not collect personal information, no personal information of yours is held on our servers. Data saved by the App stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security.

8. Your Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you have rights over your personal information. Since the App does not collect personal information, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, or delete. Your on-device data can be deleted at any time as described in Section 2.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: aymorato.apps@gmail.com
